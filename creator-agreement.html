Creator Agreement

Last updated: 1 August 2026

This Creator Agreement governs your application to and participation in the Content & Co Creator Network. By submitting your application, you agree to the terms outlined below.

01 · Non-Exclusive Representation

Joining Content & Co does not create an exclusive relationship. Creators are completely free to work with other agencies, platforms, and brand partners independently.

02 · Permission to Represent

You authorize Content & Co to present your profile, media kit, portfolio, audience metrics, and publicly available content to prospective brand partners for the purpose of securing collaboration and sponsorship opportunities.

03 · Media Kit & Assets

You grant Content & Co permission to store and use the materials you provide (including media kits, rate cards, audience insights, profile photos, logos, and portfolio links) solely for creator representation and campaign proposals.

04 · Brand Introductions

We may contact brands on your behalf and introduce you as a candidate for relevant campaigns, sponsorships, and paid partnerships.

05 · Campaign Acceptance

Every collaboration requires your explicit approval. You retain full freedom to accept or decline any opportunity presented to you.

06 · No Guarantee

Submission of your application does not guarantee acceptance into our creator network or placement in brand campaigns.

07 · Accuracy

You confirm that all information, social metrics, engagement rates, and performance statistics you provide are accurate and truthful.

08 · Removal

You may request removal from our creator network at any time by emailing us at Tafiqul@contentco.info.

09 · Payments

Campaign payments are processed transparently. Depending on the campaign agreement, payments are either disbursed directly by the brand partner or collected by Content & Co and remitted to you upon campaign completion and deliverables approval.

10 · Commission

Content & Co may receive a commission from brands for campaigns facilitated through our agency. This does not affect the creator's agreed compensation unless explicitly agreed upon in writing prior to campaign execution.

11 · Intellectual Property

Creators retain full ownership of all content, videos, and creative assets produced, subject only to the specific usage rights licensed to brands under individual campaign contracts.

12 · Privacy

Your personal data is handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
