Turning an eSIM Into Part of the Travel Story

Most eSIM campaigns ask creators to pause the trip and read a discount code. We developed a challenge-driven angle where the technology quietly enables the adventure.

“Can I Survive 48 Hours in Tokyo Without Hotel Wi-Fi?”
The Standard Problem

Standard creator integrations sound like commercials. Viewers scroll past discount codes because nobody wakes up wanting to watch someone install an eSIM.

Our Strategic Angle

Make the eSIM the invisible hero of a travel challenge. The creator navigates, translates, and books transit live. If connection fails, the trip gets harder.

Campaign Experiment Specimen 02

I Let the Car Decide Where I Go

Most car rental sponsorships are dry reviews. We structured a challenge concept for DiscoverCars.com where comparing vehicle conditions actually decides the destination.

“I Let My Rental Car Decide My Entire Trip”
The Standard Problem

Renting a car isn't the exciting part; the road trip is. Audiences skip generic sponsorships because they don't care about comparing mileage or conditions on spreadsheets.

Our Strategic Angle

A spontaneous journey built around constraints. The creator uses DiscoverCars.com to compare offers within a budget. The vehicle selected decides the road trip direction.
